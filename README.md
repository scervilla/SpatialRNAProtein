# RNA and protein are not interchangeable in single-cell spatial multi-omics

> **Authors**: S. Cervilla, D. Grases, N. Montesdeoca, A. Font, M. Fernandez-Saorin, O. Etxaniz, J. C. Pardo, M. Ochoa-de-Olza, M. Figols, N. Sala-Gonzalez, C. Fina, M. Rodriguez, J. P. de Torres, M. D. Lozano, L. M. Montuenga, C. de Andrea, R. Pio, V. Ruiz de Porras, E. Porta-Pardo

---

## Overview

This repository contains the analysis and figure scripts used in our study, which evaluates RNA–protein concordance in single-cell spatial multi-omics (Xenium gene and protein expression) across lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) and metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC).
